**PREFACE**

# A Word Before We Begin

There are a great many books written about flying combat, and most of them are too long. I have read enough of them to know. They are written by men who wanted you to understand how much they knew, and the size of the book was the proof. I have tried to write the opposite kind of book. If it seems thin in your hands, that is on purpose. Everything I have left out, I left out because it would not have kept you alive.

I am not going to pad this. I have neither the years nor the patience left for it, and you have a great deal to learn and, if the next few decades go the way the last few have started to, not as long as you would like in which to learn it. So I will say a thing once, plainly, and trust you to do the work of taking it in. A teacher who says everything three times has decided in advance that you are not paying attention. I have decided the opposite. We will see which of us is right.

Most of what is in here was paid for in blood, and not always mine. That is not a figure of speech. Every rule in the back of this book is short because the long version was a person, and the person is gone. When I tell you to keep your speed up coming out of a turn, there is a name behind it. I am not going to give you the names every time, because if I did the book would be all names and no flying, and the names would stop meaning anything, which is the one thing I will not allow to happen to them. But you should know they are there, underneath the sentences, the way the dead are always underneath the living who learned from them.

I will tell you why I am writing this now, and then I will not mention it again. A pilot I owe my life to asked me to. She is the sort of person who does not ask for much, so when she asks, you do it. And the peace we have been enjoying is beginning to feel less like peace and more like the quiet part before the loud part. I have heard that quiet before. I would rather you learned from a book than learned the way I did.

That is the whole of it. Now we can get on with the flying.
